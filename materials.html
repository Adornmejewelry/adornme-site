A little help finding your perfect fit — for necklaces, rings, and bracelets.

Choose the length that suits your neckline and how you like a piece to sit.

Bracelets

Our Pearl Bracelet is made to a standard fit with an adjustable extender. The Cuff is shaped to a standard women's wrist. If you need a custom length, note it at checkout or email us before ordering.

Still unsure?

Every piece is made to order, so we'd rather get it right the first time. Reach out at hello@adornme.ai with any sizing question and we'll guide you personally.
