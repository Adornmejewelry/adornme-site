For designers

Designer Royalty

Earn 5% each time another client orders your design, up to $150 per order.

How it works

  1. Design your piece on our Customize page: the piece, the metal (including 14K and 18K solid gold), the drop, your initial or engraving.
  2. When you order, tick "Add my design to the Adornme Gallery" and tell us the name to credit.
  3. Our studio reviews each new design. Selected designs join the Client Designs section of the Gallery, with your name.
  4. Every time another client orders your design, you earn 5% of what they paid, up to $150 per order.

Custom orders welcome

Have an idea beyond our Customize options? We take custom orders too. Tell us about it in the notes on the Customize page or at hello@adornme.ai, and we'll design it with you. Every new custom design that other clients order earns its designer the same 5% royalty.

Royalties are 5% of the price paid after any discount, not counting tax and shipping, and are paid once the 14-day return window has passed, by PayPal, Venmo or Zelle, or as Adornme store credit. Your own orders of your design don't count. Adornme chooses which designs join the Gallery and may remove a design at any time.

For sales reps & creators

Adornme Sales Partners

Earn 5% on every sale you bring, in every metal, up to $150 per order.

Your clients and followers get $30 off any order of $250 or more with your code.

How it works

  1. Apply below: creators with the online form, sales reps by email. We review every application personally.
  2. Once approved, you receive your own Adornme code and link to share.
  3. Share Adornme with your clients, friends and followers: silver, gold plated, and 14K and 18K solid gold pieces and custom designs.
  4. Every order placed with your code or through your link earns you 5%, up to $150 per order. Sales are tracked automatically and commissions are paid through Shopify Collabs.

Commission is 5% of the price paid after any discount, not counting tax and shipping, up to $150 per order. Returned orders don't earn commission. Creators must clearly mark posts about Adornme as a paid partnership (for example #ad or #AdornmePartner).

Questions

Can my design be in solid gold?

Yes. Design it in 14K or 18K solid gold, in yellow, white or rose gold. Gold designs earn the same 5%, up to $150 per order.

How will I know when someone orders my design?

We'll email you when your design joins the Gallery and each time a royalty is paid.

Can I be both a designer and a Sales Partner?

Of course. Royalties are paid on your designs; Sales Partner commission is paid on the sales you bring.

Anything else?

Write to us at hello@adornme.ai. We're a small, founder-led studio and we read every message.
